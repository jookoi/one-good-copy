Photos

One shot. Many files. Several drives.

The RAW, the camera JPEG, exports, resizes and colour edits of one shot, grouped as one photo family. They do not have to be identical files.

How photos are matched

  • Each photo's header is read: camera, capture time and pixel size. Only the header, never the whole photo.
  • Photos from the same camera with the same capture time are one shot. A RAW and its JPEG match even though not one byte is the same. So do an export and a colour edit, as long as they keep the capture time.
  • Shots taken seconds apart are shown as neighbours. They are never called copies.
  • Grouping uses the details already read, so no photo is opened twice.
  • Nothing is marked for deletion. The app never picks the JPEG over the RAW. You choose.

Check before you choose

Open a large preview of any photo. Every copy shows its folder, size, pixel size and capture time.
