How folders are matched

  • Files match on content. Size first, then a fingerprint of a small part of the file. The whole file is read only when those parts match.
  • Two folders count as copies when they share enough of their files: at least half for a big folder, more for a small one. Settings has a stricter and a looser mode.
  • A parent folder only matches when its bigger subfolders match too. Two drives that both hold one Photos folder are not called copies.
  • In Compare, files with no copy in the other columns are counted at the top, and a dash marks a file a copy is missing.
